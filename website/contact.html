Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things we get asked most.

How fast do orders ship?
Orders placed before 2pm PT leave our Portland studio the same business day; everything else ships the next. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within the US.
Are the cards blank inside?
Yes — every card is blank inside so the words can be yours. Each one comes with a matching envelope in recycled kraft or ivory.
Do you offer wholesale pricing?
We do! We partner with independent shops across the country. Send us a note with "Wholesale" selected in the form above and we'll share our catalog, pricing, and minimums.
What is your return policy?
If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you expected, email us within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you — no need to send anything back.
Can I request a custom design?
For weddings, events, and businesses we offer custom runs starting at 50 cards. Choose "Custom cards" in the contact form and tell us about your project.
