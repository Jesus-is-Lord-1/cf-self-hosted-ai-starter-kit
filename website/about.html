Our story

Cards worth keeping, made by hand

We believe a great card outlives the moment it marks. That belief has guided everything we've made since 2018.

💌
How it started

A letterpress in a garage

Everbright Cards began in a Portland garage with a secondhand letterpress, a stack of recycled paper, and one stubborn idea: greeting cards should feel as meaningful as the words written inside them.

Founder June Okafor had spent years watching beautiful moments get marked with forgettable cards. So she started drawing her own — pressing each one by hand, listing them at a neighborhood market, and selling out by noon.

Eight years later, we're a team of twelve working with independent illustrators across the country. Every card is still printed in Portland, still on recycled cotton paper, and still made to be kept.

What we stand for

Our values

Three commitments shape every card that leaves our studio.

🎨

Art first

Every design is original artwork by an independent illustrator, who earns a royalty on every single card sold.

🌱

Planet friendly

100% recycled cotton paper, soy-based inks, plastic-free packaging, and carbon-neutral shipping on every order.

🤝

Community rooted

We donate 1% of every sale to local literacy programs, because words matter — on paper and everywhere else.
