Strategy C · Rejected on Arithmetic — Shown for Discipline

Buy, hold, refinance as a rental — the math says no

Tested at the $2.15M ask with mid-2026 DSCR terms. Market rent ~$6,500/mo produces roughly $27K of NOI after non-homestead taxes ($27K), insurance ($12K), management, and reserves — a 1.24% cap rate. No coverage-based lender funds this. The refinance contingency fails the same test at 0.34 furnished.

This asset's value is a sale to an owner-occupant who prices schools and walkability — not an income stream. Rejecting this openly protects investor capital from a "we'll just rent it out" fallback that doesn't exist.

Strategy D · Negotiation Fallback Only

Creative structures: trade our price for their terms

The builder is likely paying 9–13% on construction debt. If they refuse $1.75M cash, offer terms that solve their carry problem while capping our basis — for almost no capital.

Lease-option
  • 24-month option at a $1.85M strike
  • ~$8,500/mo lease · $1,500/mo credited to purchase
  • Control the asset for ~$25K option money instead of ~$800K of equity
  • Exercise only if the spring market confirms the exit
Seller carry
  • $1.90M price — builder holds a 75% note
  • 5–6% interest-only, 18 months — below our 9.5% bridge cost
  • The above-market price is financed below market — roughly offsetting itself
  • Builder converts dead carry into interest income

Presented as negotiation instruments, not the plan of record — returns depend on final terms and are bounded by the same comp band as Strategies A and B.
