The house is not the problem: 6bd/5.5ba new construction (2025), 0.6 acre, a two-minute walk to Wills Park, six minutes to downtown Alpharetta and Avalon, Milton High School district (10/10). The problem was a launch price of $526/sqft that only Avalon-adjacent product has ever achieved. The market said no for a year — and a fatigued seller is how good basis gets bought.

Slide 03 · Why the mispricing exists

The seller has been carrying this project for 21 months

Lot purchased October 2024 for $570K, construction through 2025, then a failed listing at $2.695M, a broker change — the builder now self-lists — and a cut to $2.15M. Spec builders carry construction debt at 9–13%; every additional month costs this seller roughly $15–20K. Sellers in that position trade price for certainty of close.

Our offer is that certainty: $1,750,000, bridge financing pre-arranged, 21-day close, no financing contingency theater. If the seller won't meet the number, we walk — the same discipline protects your capital after we own it.

We tested refinance-and-hold honestly: the asset cannot cover debt service as a rental (0.34 DSCR). So we don't promise it. The bear case prices a sale at the $2.05M comp floor instead.

Slide 07 · Structure

Slide 08 · Alignment

How we make money — and when we don't

No acquisition fee. No asset-management fee. No disposition fee. The GP is compensated exclusively by the 30% promote above your 8% preferred return, and the GP's own $79K sits in first-loss position. In the bear case the GP loses real money while LP capital comes back whole. In the base case the GP earns ~$32K on $79K invested — meaningful only because your outcome was met first.

Discipline you can hold us to: the offer is $1,750,000 and the ceiling is $1,800,000. If the seller says no, this deal dies and your capital is never called. The next mispriced listing gets the same model.
