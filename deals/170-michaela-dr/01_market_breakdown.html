Synthesis

Two things are true at once

The market is strong
  • 2.3 months of supply — still seller-friendly
  • Well-priced $2M+ homes clear in 1–2.5 months
  • ~36% of homes still sell over list
  • Income, jobs, and schools keep feeding the buyer pool
Buyers stopped overpaying
  • 30009 $/sq ft down 4.6% year over year
  • List-to-sale slipping to ~97%
  • Aspirational pricing ($500+/sq ft off-Avalon) sits unsold
  • The subject is the case study: −20% and still active

That split — strong absorption, disciplined pricing — is precisely the environment where buying a mispriced asset below the band and selling it inside the band works.
