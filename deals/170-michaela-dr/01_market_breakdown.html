Why this listing failed — and why that's the entry.
The builder has carried this project for 21 months: land purchase Oct 2024, construction through 2025, and nearly a year of marketing — likely on a construction loan costing 9–10%. Every month of delay costs the seller roughly $15–20K in interest and carry. Sellers in that position trade price for certainty.
