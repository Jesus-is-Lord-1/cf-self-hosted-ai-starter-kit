Regulatory reality (Ordinance No. 881, in force since Jan 2025): STRs are legal in Alpharetta single-family homes with an annual city license — but licenses are capped at 5% of homes per neighborhood, no two STRs may be adjacent on the same street, 8% lodging tax applies, parking must be 1 off-street space per bedroom, and renewals are due each Jan 31. Your license is therefore not guaranteed — and a $2.15M asset earning ~2.5% doesn't justify the operational load even if granted.

Why holds fail here in one sentence: rent scales with bedrooms, but this asset's value scales with finish and location — so at $2.5M of value and ~$132K of achievable rent, every hold structure is buying a bond that yields less than its own debt costs.

Personal residence wildcard B

If you (or a buyer in your network) want to live in downtown Alpharetta: buying at $1.82M + $173K completion = $1.99M basis on a home worth ~$2.4–2.55M finished — roughly $400–500K of move-in equity, top Fulton schools (Milton HS feeder), walkable to Main Street and the Alpha Loop. A 20% jumbo down (~$420K including completion cash) at today's rates carries it for roughly what a comparable rental would cost, with the equity as the prize. This is the one "hold" that works, because it's priced against comps, not cap rates.

2 · Quick-exit strategies — the spread test

Wholesale / assignment D

An end flipper underwriting honestly (72–75% of a $2.5M ARV, minus $173K completion) can pay at most ≈ $1.63–1.70M. Your ask is $1.82M — there is no assignable spread at these numbers. A wholesale deal here only "works" by selling the $3.0M ARV story to a less-informed buyer, which risks blown closings and your reputation.

Novation agreement B+

Don't buy it — partner with the seller: you fund the $173K completion + staging + carry (~$215K total), control the listing, and split everything above the seller's $1.82M floor.

No acquisition closing costs, no hard-money points, no transfer tax — the highest ROI structure tested. Requires a solvent, cooperative seller, ironclad written agreement (memorandum recorded, funds through escrow, deed-in-escrow or performance mortgage as security), and clean title. If the seller is a distressed builder LLC, this is often exactly what they want.

Flip with seller financing A−

Trade price for terms: offer $1.87M with the seller carrying 90% at 6% interest-only, 12-month balloon. Versus hard money you skip ~$33K in points and save ~4.5% in rate — worth ≈ $80K against a $50K price concession.

Bottom line across all eight tests. 170 Michaela is a finish-and-sell asset.
